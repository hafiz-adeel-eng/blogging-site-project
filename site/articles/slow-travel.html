Take the scenic route. Stay a little longer.

Plan a slower trip with fewer destinations, local context and time to explore. Review transport, entry requirements and practical travel essentials.

dvDailyVertex Editorial1 min read

A practical guide to slower travel.

Make room in the itinerary

Choose fewer destinations and leave some hours unplanned. Staying longer in one place reduces time spent in transit and gives you more opportunities to understand daily life.

Travel with local context

Read local guidance, respect customs, and check opening times and transport options. Choose independent businesses where practical and ask before photographing people.

Prepare the essentials

Check current entry requirements, insurance coverage, and safety advice before booking. Keep offline copies of important information. Slower travel still benefits from careful preparation.
