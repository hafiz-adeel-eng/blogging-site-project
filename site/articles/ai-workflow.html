The future of work is human. With a little help from AI.

Build a thoughtful AI workflow: choose a useful task, verify generated answers, protect sensitive information, and compare results with your current process.

dvDailyVertex Editorial1 min read

Building a thoughtful AI workflow.

Start with a real problem

Choose one repetitive task: organizing research, summarizing your own notes, or drafting an outline. Define what a useful result looks like before you choose a tool. This gives you something concrete to evaluate.

Keep a person in the loop

Treat generated answers as drafts. Check facts against original sources, remove confidential information from prompts, and review the final output before sharing it. For consequential decisions, consult a qualified professional.

Build a repeatable process

Save a prompt that explains your goal, context, and constraints. Compare the result with your current process, recording time saved and corrections needed. Keep the tool only when it improves the work.
