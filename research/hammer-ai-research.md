## Original explanatory value

- Distinguish a release repository and automatic source archives from the full application's source availability.
- Record the actual installer architecture evidence, especially arm64 Mac versus an unsupported assumption about Intel compatibility.
- Separate the application download from model downloads, memory use and inference performance.
- Explain why a local model does not prove the complete application is offline or that no telemetry or backups exist.
- Provide an original, non-sensitive character-writing prompt and reproducible evaluation worksheet without fabricated results.
- Keep current prices, product-specific privacy conclusions and performance ratings out of unverified factual claims.

These are coverage choices and evidence gaps, not claims that every competitor omits them.
